# Rolling Motion

> Version 1.0.0
> Supports Cavalry 2.6.0 and later.
> **[Buy Now](https://app.lava.top/products/b5d33a27-8315-4fae-b9b0-878a0fffcd1b "Go to the store")**
{: .py-3 .bg-gray .rounded-2 }

This plugin is a Deformer Layer that rotates the Shape and moves it, simulating rolling along a flat surface.

## User Manual

**Strength** - Determine the strength of the Deformer.

**Roll Angle** - Angle of rotation in degrees.

**High Quality** - Geometry will be sampled to a higher quality, at the expense of performance.
